# TRUST UNCLE RED

> He told you how to escape. Why would you trust him?

**TRUST UNCLE RED** is a short 2D puzzle-platformer built around the theme **"TRUST NO ONE."**

Uncle Red gives you instructions to escape a mysterious 3-floor building.

The problem?

**His instructions aren't always what they seem.**

Sometimes you need to follow what he says.
Sometimes you need to question it.
And sometimes... you just need to push Uncle Red.

---

## Gameplay

The game takes place across **3 floors**, with each floor having its own puzzle.

You'll encounter:

- Platforming challenges
- Buttons and switches
- Falling spikes
- Hidden solutions
- Misleading instructions
- Uncle Red

The main idea is simple:

**Don't blindly trust the person telling you what to do.**

## Visuals

The game was built using mostly simple Unity primitives instead of a large collection of external assets.

Sometimes you don't need fancy assets.

You just need cubes, spikes, questionable level design, and an uncle you definitely shouldn't trust.

---

## Development

This was made as a game jam project under a tight time limit.

The goal was to keep the mechanics simple while making the **TRUST NO ONE** theme part of the actual gameplay instead of just putting it in the game's description.
